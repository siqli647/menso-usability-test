UX/UI Frictions

1. An enabled policy allowed a matching action through in silence.
Show an enforcement outcome on every execution — 'Blocked pending approval', 'Allowed: no matching policy', or 'Allowed: exception' — naming the policy that matched. Silence is indistinguishable from having no control at all.
2. The Approvals queue and the policy state disagree with each other.
Zero pending items while the policy reads active is a governance defect, not a UI gap. Validate policy-to-action matching across every Playground and trigger path, and treat silent pass-through as release-blocking.
3. Executions have no terminal state.
The run stayed 'in progress' indefinitely, so the operator could not tell whether a gate was holding it or nothing was. Every execution needs a final, visible status.
4. Proving one control required building the whole environment first.
Ship a seeded compliance sandbox with sample integrations, actions and audit history so an evaluator can verify enforcement without assembling an organisation, workspace and policy by hand.
