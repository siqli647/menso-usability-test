UX/UI Frictions

1. No security, trust or compliance destination exists on the public site.
Publish a trust center in the top nav covering certifications (or an honest 'in progress' status), encryption in transit and at rest, audit-log retention, SSO and role controls. This is the cheapest of the three fixes and the one that unblocks the buying conversation.
2. The Zapier comparison has no governance row.
The table covers OAuth, MCP, SDKs and workspace isolation but skips audit, approvals and certifications — the exact rows a compliance-driven switcher reads first. Add them, even where the answer is 'roadmap'.
3. Pricing names features without saying which tier enforces them.
Map governance capability to tiers explicitly: which plan enforces approvals, which exports audit logs, which includes SSO. Without it a buyer cannot self-qualify before contacting sales.
4. The strongest governance evidence is buried in docs.
The human-gates guide explains the approval model convincingly, but it took 21 steps to reach. Link it from pricing and from the comparison page where the question is actually asked.
