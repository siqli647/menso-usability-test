Where Synter made Declan work

MAJORSteps 16–18
1. The most prominent card in the connections grid doesn't answer clicks.
“The card grid is right in front of me. I'll try the Google Ads card by its label this time — I want to see the actual connect flow and permissions it requests.”
Declan opened Connections and found the catalog he came for — ten ad platforms, his agency's whole roster. He clicked Google Ads, the card any evaluator tries first. Nothing. He clicked it again by its label, watching for any response. Nothing. On the third pass he stopped fighting the card and finished through the Connect account button at the top of the page.
Why this costs trust
The first interactive proof of the product's core promise went silent under the evaluator's cursor. A power user forgives one dead control, but this is the moment doubt entered an otherwise clean execution story — on the highest-traffic card in the grid.
Actor friction note · Step 16 · verbatim
The most prominent card in the grid (Google Ads) doesn't respond to direct clicks the way the other platform cards do — as a power user I'd expect every card in a uniform grid to behave identically, and this inconsistency slows me down.
What to restore
Make every card in the grid uniformly clickable — or visibly non-interactive — and add an interaction test for the primary card so a silent regression here cannot ship.
Evidence boundary: Observed as repeated unanswered clicks on the Google Ads card in this run while the page's primary button worked; other cards were not systematically tried.
