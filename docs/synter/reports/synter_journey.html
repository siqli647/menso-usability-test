Where Synter converts — and where the grid breaks trust

MAJORSteps 14–17
1. Identical cards, non-identical behaviour: the Google Ads tile stayed silent.
“The card grid is right in front of me. I'll try the Google Ads card by its label this time — I want to see the actual connect flow and permissions it requests.”
The Connections grid presents ten ad platforms as one uniform row of cards. Declan clicked the Google Ads card three times and nothing happened; the same gesture on the primary 'Connect account' button worked immediately. His words: he expects every card in a uniform grid to behave identically — that is what a grid promises.
Why this costs trust
This is the first interaction after the aha moment, on the biggest ad network in the list. A dead click there reads as 'the integration layer is flaky', which is precisely the doubt an execution product cannot afford.
Actor friction note · Step 16 · verbatim
The most prominent card in the grid (Google Ads) doesn't respond to direct clicks the way the other platform cards do — as a power user I'd expect every card in a uniform grid to behave identically, and this inconsistency slows me down.
What to restore
Make the whole card the click target for every platform, or make it visibly non-interactive with the action living only on the button.
Evidence boundary: Three attempts in one session; our clicks were delivered to the page and produced no visible change. Whether a human mouse would hit a different hit-box on that card is not established.
MINORSteps 10–12
2. The strongest screen in the product is the one right after login.
“I'm in — free tier with $25 of monthly usage, and there's already a 'Run in Claude' MCP snippet visible. First, clearing this webinar popup so I can actually read the workspace.”
The dashboard's 'Run in Claude' card shows the hosted MCP endpoint — mcp.syntermedia.ai over HTTP — with install paths for Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. Declan recognised it instantly as the thing he would plug into his own stack. There is no hunting, no setup wizard, no sales call.
Why this costs trust
Keep this. For a developer-evaluator this single card does the work an onboarding flow usually fails to do.
Actor friction note · Step 10 · verbatim
A webinar promo popup covers part of the dashboard immediately on first login — as someone here to evaluate the MCP/API layer, this is pure noise and adds a step before I can see the product.
What to restore
Protect the endpoint card's position as the product grows, and consider surfacing it pre-login for developers evaluating before signup.
Evidence boundary: One developer persona; non-technical buyers were not represented.
MAJORSteps 17–18
3. Every path to data runs through authorising a live ad account.
“This is the full picture I needed: ten ad platforms ready to connect via OAuth, an API key already active, and the MCP install flow I saw earlier. Connecting a real account means handing off to Google or Meta OAuth, which is beyond what I'd do in a first look. Technically, this covers my cross-platform reporting use case — I'd trial the MCP read tier next.”
The connect sheet offers OAuth into a real ad account or a fifteen-minute call to have it connected for you. Declan stopped there, deliberately: handing over production ad credentials is beyond what he would do in a first look. The evaluation ended with the integration story understood and the product's actual output — campaign data through MCP — unseen.
Why this costs trust
Evaluators who will not connect production accounts on day one cannot reach the value the product is sold on. That is a large fraction of careful technical buyers.
Actor friction note · Step 18 · verbatim
The Google Ads card in the platform grid never responded to clicks while other cards did — a small but noticeable inconsistency for a power user evaluating execution-layer reliability.
What to restore
Ship a demo workspace or read-only sample account so a first session can run one real MCP query — list platforms, pull sample performance — before any OAuth.
Evidence boundary: He explored the overview, install page, campaign IDE and Connections; a sample-data path living elsewhere would not have been seen. Stopping was his choice, not a block.
