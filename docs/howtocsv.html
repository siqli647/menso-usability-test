Cross-Persona Takeaways

The public cleaner path is discoverable.

All three personas found Quick CSV Cleaner, loaded sample data, imported it, ran Auto-Cleaner, and reached an output or export state without signup.

Privacy copy is doing real trust work.

Local-processing language reassured both operations and business users before they risked sample or supplier-style data.

Technical adoption depends on audit evidence.

The same flow that Miguel would adopt was rejected by Hannah because the product did not show before/after diffs, changed-cell highlights, or rule counts.
