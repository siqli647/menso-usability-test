Cross-Persona Takeaways

Private student help is the strongest trust signal.

Both student personas moved forward when anonymity was explicit and the workflow let them ask without public embarrassment.

AI Tutor is stronger than inline Q&A answers.

Alex and Sofia both saw generic Q&A responses first, then found the real value after switching to the dedicated tutor flow.

Teacher adoption depends on control proof.

Denise liked the lesson-grounded signals, but unclickable Respond controls and missing moderation affordances create serious classroom risk.
