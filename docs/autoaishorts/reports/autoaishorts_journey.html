Where AutoAIShorts lost Renata

MAJORSteps 2–8
1. The front door only knows new users.
“Ugh, I've been poking at this sign-up form and getting nowhere. Wait — I actually already have an account here, I don't need to sign up again. There's a Sign In link at the bottom, that's my way in.”
Renata clicked 'Create My First Video Free' the way any warm visitor does — and landed on a sign-up form. She already had an account, said so immediately, and went looking for the way back in. The only sign-in affordance is a small, low-contrast link tucked below the form — beneath the fold at her viewport — with no 'Already have an account?' prompt anywhere near the fields she was staring at.
Why this costs trust
Returning users are the warmest traffic a creator tool has. The primary CTA routes them into account creation and hides the way back, so the paying-user re-entry path depends on spotting footer fine print.
Actor friction note · Step 12 · verbatim
The sign-up page doesn't surface the 'already have an account? Sign in' option anywhere near the form itself — it's buried as a tiny footer link. As someone in a hurry, I expect that toggle right under the Create Account button.
What to restore
Add an 'Already have an account? Sign in' link directly beside the form's primary button, and send recognized return visits to sign-in instead of sign-up.
Evidence boundary: Observed on the sign-up page as rendered this session; whether the landing CTA adapts for cookied returning visitors in other states was not tested.
MAJORSteps 12–15
2. Eighteen steps never escaped the sign-up form.
“Ugh, this create-account form keeps eating my clicks and going nowhere — and I actually already have an account here. There's a Sign In link at the bottom, so I'm switching to that instead of fighting this form.”
From step two to the budget's end, every attempt to reach sign-in ended back on the same 'Create account' card. The session's clicks kept landing in the form's fields — the full-name input took focus again and again — while the page gave no feedback, no error, and no route correction. She named the trap out loud twice: the form 'keeps eating my clicks and going nowhere.'
Why this costs trust
A motivated returning user with correct credentials spent an entire session on the wrong form. Whatever share of that is her aim and whatever share is the page's layout, the product converted its warmest visitor into a bounce.
Actor friction note · Step 15 · verbatim
The Create Account page gave zero feedback across many attempts — no validation message, no error, nothing changed on screen. As someone who tests tools fast, a dead signup form this early would normally make me bail on the product entirely.
What to restore
Make the sign-in switch a first-class control inside the form card, and consider an inline notice when an existing account's email is typed into sign-up.
Evidence boundary: The recorded clicks focused form fields rather than the footer link, so whether the 'Sign In' link itself navigates when precisely hit was not established in this run; a follow-up session entering via /sign-in directly is noted in the journey page.
