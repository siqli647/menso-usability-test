Final Verdict

Where AutoAIShorts loses a new creator

CRITICALSteps 10–12
1. The advertised feature is the one a new account cannot run.
“Oh wow, this studio is actually cute — script writer, styles, voice, captions all in one place. But 'needs 13 credits, you have 0' is a red flag. Let me still drop my productivity tip idea in and see how far it lets me go.”
The landing page sells 'faceless AI videos' and '2 free videos a day, no credit card'. Oksana signed up, opened the Faceless Video studio the sidebar points at, wrote her prompt, and hit Engage Generation — and the studio asked for 13 credits against a balance of 0. The click produced a flash of 'Loading Create Studio' and nothing else: no dialog, no explanation, no route to the free entitlement she had just been promised.
Why this costs trust
This is the first real action a new user takes, on the feature the homepage advertises. A silent dead click there reads as 'broken', not 'paid' — and the promise that brought them in is the thing that fails.
Actor friction note · Step 11 · verbatim
Seeing 'needs 13 credits — you have 0' before I've even made one video kills the 'test quality before paying' promise — as a creator I want at least one free render to judge voice and visual quality.
What to restore
If the flagship generator is credit-gated for new accounts, say so before the prompt is written, and put the free path (Reddit Stories) on the same screen. A blocked click must explain itself.
Evidence boundary: One freshly registered account on this date. Whether other signup routes grant starting credits was not tested; the balance shown was 0 and the studio quoted 13.
MAJORSteps 15–17
2. The free path rewrote her idea into someone else's story.
“Wait, it turned my 2-minute rule tip into a whole Reddit story about 'Mike the programmer' — kinda clever actually. 52 seconds, scenes and captions are set up. Live preview is still initializing, so let me hit Render / Export and see the actual video.”
She found 'Reddit Stories — 2 FREE' in the sidebar and pasted her own productivity tip into it. What came back was a first-person Reddit narrative about 'Mike the programmer' — 52 seconds, scenes and captions already laid out. She found it clever, and it was still not her content: the free template did not just restyle her idea, it replaced the narrator and the story.
Why this costs trust
A creator evaluating an AI video tool is judging control as much as quality. Silent rewriting of intent is the fastest way to lose the users who care most about their own voice.
Actor friction note · Step 17 · verbatim
The free flow hijacked my productivity tip into a fictional Reddit story format — as a creator I'd want control over that framing, not an automatic 'Mike in San Francisco' narrative.
What to restore
Show the generated script before rendering with an obvious edit step, and label the Reddit format as a narrative transform rather than a style.
Evidence boundary: One prompt through one free format; whether the paid Faceless flow preserves the original voice was never observable on this account.
MAJORSteps 24–27
3. The render finished, but the product never said where the result went.
“Oh nice, it's actually rendering now — 27% and climbing with 'Processor Active' on screen. This is the part where I just let it cook and see if the final video's export-ready for Shorts.”
Her mental model was press export, watch progress, get a file. Instead the button disappeared, a toast flashed 'Video generation started', the view bounced between a progress screen and the studio, and no persistent indicator survived the transition. She only confirmed the finished 52-second video by going to My Videos herself.
Why this costs trust
The moment of delivery is the moment a trial converts. Losing the user between 'started' and 'ready' turns a successful render into an anxious hunt.
Actor friction note · Step 26 · verbatim
Render times matter a lot for my daily posting schedule — if this takes forever for a 54-second clip, that's a real bottleneck for high-volume creators like me.
What to restore
Keep one persistent render status with a link to the finished asset, and land the user on the result rather than back in the studio.
Evidence boundary: Observed on one render in one session; queue behaviour under load was not exercised.
