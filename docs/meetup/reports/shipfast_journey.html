UX/UI Frictions

1. As a solo developer evaluating a code boilerplate, Sam expected licensing to be explicit before purchase.
Add a dedicated FAQ item in the FAQ section labeled with licensing language such as "Can I use ShipFast for multiple projects or client apps?" and place it near other purchase-decision questions.
2. Sam wanted a quick tier comparison before investing more attention, but the pricing layout required additional…
In the pricing section, place the key differences between Starter and All-in near the top of the cards or add a compact comparison summary above the longer feature lists.
3. After deciding he had enough information, Sam expected the purchase entry point to be immediately accessible from…
Move each plan's buy button higher within the pricing cards or repeat the CTA at both the top and bottom of the card on the pricing section.
