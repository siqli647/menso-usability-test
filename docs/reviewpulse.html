03 · Journey findings

Strength

Observed: The empty Customers page exposed a clear Add customer action. The required-field rule was understandable, and the record appeared after submit.

“The add flow was quick, no CSV needed, just name and email. Decision: adopt.”

P1 · Certainty

Submit success relied on noticing the list change

Observed: The interface returned to the list and showed the new row without an explicit success message.

Implication: When adding multiple customers, a business owner may hesitate or submit twice because the system did not clearly acknowledge the action.

Recommendation: Show a brief success message naming the added customer and keep the new row visually highlighted.

P2 · Focus

Optional details compete with a 30-second task

Observed: Service type, date, source, and notes were all present even though the first customer required only a name and one contact method.

Implication: The form looks heavier than the happy path actually is.

Recommendation: Keep required fields visible and place the rest under a “More details” disclosure.
